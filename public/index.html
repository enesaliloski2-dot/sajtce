Security

Read before connecting

Never share a seed phrase

The Relief Division will never ask for your 12 or 24 word phrase, your password, or a payment to unlock funds. Only a network signature is requested.

Questions

Reference

Is the $5,000 taxable?

Treated as non-wage income. Consult a preparer for your own filing position.

Which wallets work?

MetaMask, Rabby, Trust, Coinbase Wallet, Rainbow, any WalletConnect wallet, plus Solana and Bitcoin.

Why is gas on my side?

The payment is broadcast from your wallet. Keep a few dollars of ETH; the amount itself is not reduced.

How long until it lands?

Routing finishes in the same session. Exchange credits can lag.

Can I claim twice?

One allocation per household. A repeat from the same address is rejected.

What if I close the tab?

Reopen and reconnect the same wallet — the claim resumes.
